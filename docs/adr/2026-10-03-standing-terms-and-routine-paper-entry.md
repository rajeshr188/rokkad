# Standing terms and routine paper entry

Lakshmi records paper advances, total-only receipts and closure, then monitors loan
health. Requiring standing agreement fields on every entry and treating transaction
confirmation as a complete-book attestation makes that supported workflow harder
than necessary. The owner authorized UR-15–18 and confirmed a 12-month standard
tenure; imported and paper loans primarily use Flexible Partial Payment.

Resolve agreement defaults with the existing original-date, series/license/Workspace
selectors. Add optional standard tenure to the existing economic policy, within
1–600 months. It defaults new entry only; saved contracts remain unchanged. Resolve
current monitoring separately. Prefer Flexible Partial Payment only when one eligible
version is unambiguous; other supported products remain explicit choices.

The ordinary paper route shows facts, jewellery and a calculated agreement summary.
Supported actual differences, unavailable dated digital setup and additional payout
details use a collapsed exception section. Unsupported fee/calculation setup must
be explained, never silently simplified. Server-side resolution binds final signed
review; changed setup requires fresh review. Original prices and approval evidence
are not manufactured. Paper proceeds remain distinct from confirmed physical cash.

Routine entry confirms the displayed transaction facts, without asserting every
paper transaction is entered. Optional explicit book verification still records an
immutable checked-through claim. Recording an unverified closure does not create
that claim. Qualified archive admission continues to require complete reconciliation.

Loan health and dashboard snapshot contract V5 retain usable calculated amounts
from entered records, with provisional and unavailable counts and completeness
labels. Known missing activity still prompts reconciliation. Other verification
statuses remain visible without blocking ordinary viewing, receipts or closure.
Reminders and recovery actions retain their existing explicit coverage gates. Closed
loans remain outside active exposure; a transaction date is never a book-check date.

This updates the presentation and routine entry semantics of the
[transaction coverage decision](2026-10-03-loan-transaction-completeness.md), while
retaining its immutable review, authorization and downstream protection boundaries.
