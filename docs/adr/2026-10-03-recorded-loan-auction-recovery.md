# Use agreed debt for current auctions of recorded loans

A recorded origin is eligible for the ordinary current auction workflow after
its transactions are confirmed complete through today. Initiation, start and
completion recheck coverage under the loan lock. Existing overdue, vault-custody,
administrator and statutory-readiness checks remain applicable. No notice is sent
automatically by this integration and historical sales are not fabricated.

Completion recognizes only the outstanding anniversary-interest delta from the
actual retained principal history, then posts the existing canonical full-debt
auction recovery, schedule termination, disposal and storage removal. It freezes
the collection basis, prior coverage review and recognition-event identity in the
recovery payload. It does not create a fictional finalized calendar period.
Scheduled allocation capacity remains separate from agreed variable-principal debt.
The completed current action appends a coverage checkpoint for the closed loan.

Coupled auction reversal restores financial and custody evidence and compensates
that exact recognition event. It resumes the original agreement, without erasing
the sale or resetting the loan anniversary. Standalone paper-origin event reversal
remains blocked. Reversal requires rechecking the active paper book before further
auction decisions. Recovery still requires exact full debt; shortfall write-off,
surplus distribution and imported-opening auctions are outside this existing
profile and require separately specified workflows.
