# Shared financial correction by agreement and history boundary

The owner approved the recommended sequence: a common agreement correction
contract, paper and direct monthly correction, downstream acceptance, opening
checkpoint correction, then connected reconciliation. Original mistakes are
different from actual subsequent payments, refunds, amendments or movements.

Keep one PawnLoan and ordinary Django services. One signed preview/confirmation
contract classifies the frozen agreement, available history and affected
dependencies. Retain financial recognition adapters where persisted evidence
differs. Do not convert direct lending into paper admission to enable correction,
invent historical approval, overwrite completed evidence, or apply today's setup
to a saved agreement.

Corrected direct terms require an explicit corrected disbursal snapshot retaining
the prior snapshot and genuine approval as provenance, not approval of revised
terms. Monthly calculation revisions retain prior accruals and lines; compensation
and new recognition must not double-count a period. Actual receipt amounts/dates
remain distinct from revised allocation. Supporting evidence and the authorized
actor bind the reviewed original proceeds. No new payout or refund is inferred.

Opening corrections distinguish original principal, cutover remaining principal,
current-period bases, recognized/unpaid interest and advance coverage. Their
original event remains the sole financial origin. Missing pre-cutover transactions
cannot be reconstructed from a changed balance. Connected changes require an
explicit supported review of the affected agreements and custody/cash facts;
unsupported graphs remain blocked rather than bypassed.

Preview and confirmation must use the same calculation, lock the affected graph,
reject stale reviews and support exactly-once retries. Retain original documents,
new-copy correction disclosure, source-local portable references and exact recovery.
Existing published profiles keep their meaning. Monitoring policy is unchanged;
changed financial facts invalidate assessments and require ordinary refresh.

Implementation and verification proceed in the ordered plan. No production loan
correction, data conversion or deployment is implied by implementation work.
