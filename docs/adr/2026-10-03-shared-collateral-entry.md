# Shared collateral entry and explicit paper receipt allocations

The owner authorized the shared creation experience. Entry purpose is a standing
default resolved from current series, license and Workspace setup, with an explicit
action-level override. Missing defaults preserve direct entry. The preference is
presentation/routing only; it cannot authorize a payout, relax direct approval or
change any existing contract. Reuse dated economic configuration scopes and their
immutable revision/audit pattern rather than adding a separate settings framework.

The 3 October UR-23 refinement uses the ordinary Series field and a compact
Entry/Change control. Do not add a duplicate series picker or separate primary
mode navigation. Changing purpose retains common item facts and each mode's typed
details; JavaScript retains actual file inputs without uploading them on a change.
A normal form POST also supports typed-fact retention without JavaScript, with
explicit photograph reselection. The carried form cache is untrusted presentation
state. It cannot save records, confer approval or reuse a signed paper review.
Normal financial submissions use their explicit purpose even if setup changed.
Retain the existing financial validators and services behind the shared experience.

Reuse the ordinary collateral editor and item-level financial evidence for paper
origination. Each item records its actual principal and metal rate; the owner
confirmed that total-only original principal is not a needed case. Sum principal,
rounded item monthly interest and advance deductions exactly. Original business
date remains distinct from recording time and current monitoring. No original
market price or digital approval is invented.

Paper receipts retain their total and derived interest/principal split. When more
than one item has outstanding principal, staff specify the item principal amounts.
Validate the exact total, item membership, per-item balance limits and currency
precision under lock; bind allocations to signed review and retry identity. This
is an explicit paper action rule, not a change to direct repayment's existing
highest-rate-first rule. Item rates and reductions govern subsequent anniversary
interest. Preserve recorded-anniversary/1 semantics on existing loans and version
the new itemized calculation profile.

Multi-item closure, correction/replay and monitoring must work before exposing
creation. Corrections preserve original item facts/events; changed allocations
require a fresh supported review. Existing direct loans and approval evidence stay
unchanged. No arbitrary equal, weight-based or price-based historical split is added.
