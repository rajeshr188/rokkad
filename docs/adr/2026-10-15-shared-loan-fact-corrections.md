# Correct confirmed loan facts by impact, independently of entry method

The owner approved one editing rule for direct and historical loan admission.
Preparation drafts remain editable; changing an approved draft requires renewed
approval. Confirmed facts use explicit reviewed corrections, while real subsequent
payments, movements, renewals and amendments remain business actions.

Replace the intermediate correction chooser with contextual navigation on loan
detail. Retain existing financial reconciliation/reversal services and their
dependency checks; navigation consolidation does not make those algorithms equal.

Add an ordinary signed-review service for transcription mistakes in an existing
collateral item's description, quantity, metal, gross/net grams and purity.
Use immutable LoanChangeLog before/after evidence and update the current item
projection. Do not alter item identity, item membership, loan state, custody,
principal, rate, fees, approval, payout or historical transaction evidence.
Known quantity/gross weight cannot silently become unknown. Unchanged inputs do
not create corrections. The same service supports active and closed agreements
with consistent financial evidence; connected renewal, funding and auction facts
require connected reconciliation before independent item correction.

Measurement/quantity/metal corrections invalidate existing appraisal applicability
through the highest appraisal version present at review. A later approved
appraisal can establish current evidence; a description typo does not invalidate
valuation. Current calculated values use corrected measurements. Existing risk
snapshots become stale, with ordinary source fingerprints preventing stale
reminders. No monitoring threshold, interest calculation or principal changes.

Original documents stay addressable. New loan copies disclose reviewed corrections
and project corrected collateral without rewriting the frozen source agreement.
Portable servicing version 6 retains the correction chain and remaps item IDs;
versions 1–5 keep their published meanings and refuse this evidence. Exact recovery
retains the same immutable rows. Correction evidence prevents unsafe downgrade.

Adding/removing items and connected graph correction are explicit unsupported
reconciliation boundaries, not draft editing or synthetic collateral movements.
