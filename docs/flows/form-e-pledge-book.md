# Form E pledge-book staff guide

**Delivery:** deployed on 30 September 2026. Open
**Reports > Statutory forms & notices > Form E books & daily activity**.
The same instructions are included in the Workspace statutory workflow guide.

## Open the physical book once

A Workspace administrator selects a series (which identifies its licence), a book
label, either A4 layout, the first pledge date and first physical page number.
Describe the earlier paper book and how the starting date avoids duplication.
Check a physical sample for binding margin, text and handwriting space, then
acknowledge opening. These settings are fixed. Do not open a new book as a way to
reprint existing pledges. A series has only one book in this increment.

Landscape prints all columns on one sheet. Facing portrait prints Left and Right
on separate sheets filed together; these receive two consecutive page numbers.
Both normally fit up to five loans. Long particulars reduce capacity or continue
onto extra pages. The separate evidence appendix does not consume book numbers.

## Review pending pledges and missing evidence

Only supported paid-out/opening records become candidates; unpaid drafts and
approvals do not. Closed operational loans remain eligible. The queue starts at
the book's chosen date and excludes loans already assigned to a saved batch.
It shows the earliest 100 pending entries and the total count; work through these
batches in order. No date-based insertion changes already issued pages.

For each entry, an administrator opens **Review evidence** and checks the original
ticket or paper-book reference. Enter missing original name/address, ownership
declaration, agreed period or identified article/value/rate supplements only when
supported. Leave unestablished facts unknown and explain the gap and follow-up.
Today's Party address or metal price is not proof of the original fact. Known
frozen values cannot be overwritten. Reviews and supplements remain traceable to
the signed-in administrator and source reference; earlier reviews remain visible.
A subsequent review supplies the complete new supplement set, not a patch to it.

**Source reviewed** means reviewed, not complete. Unknowns and warnings remain in
the saved PDF. Unsupported origin/renewal evidence and ambiguous payout attempts
remain blocked pending reconciliation. Archived closed records without normalized
licence/series mappings are counted separately and excluded. Do not claim the
output reconstructs the complete historical pledge book.

## Save, print and file

1. Select an activity cutoff and **Full pages only** or **Include last partial page**.
2. Preview the PDF and review the selected entries, evidence and next page number.
3. Confirm **Save permanent print batch**. Source changes or another operator's
   saved batch require a fresh review. Retrying the same successful request returns
   the saved batch; it does not duplicate entries or consume another page.
4. Download **Print / exact reprint PDF** under Saved batches. Print A4, actual
   size (100%), single-sided in the saved orientation; file the pages and notes.
5. Close outstanding partial pages during daily work rather than waiting for a
   full page. A partial saved page stays closed; do not add new pledges in its
   unused space or feed it through the printer to append later pledges.

Saving does not establish that paper was printed, signed or filed. If the printer
fails, download the exact saved batch again. A late-recorded earlier pledge takes
the next available page with its true business date; earlier numbers never move.

## Update older entries by hand

Continue normal payment/release/auction recording in Rokkad. Open **Daily activity /
handwritten updates**, usually filtering by **Date entered in Rokkad**, to catch
yesterday's closure entered today. Actual business dates and recording timestamps
both appear. The business-date filter supports reconciliation for a chosen day.

The list covers collections, partial/full releases, auction recovery, reversals,
renewal warnings and new payouts. Amounts retain their breakdown and concessions
are not counted as cash. It supplies saved page ranges and available recipient
details. An event already captured at the batch cutoff is labelled. If there is no
saved reference, check the earlier paper book or pending queue instead of guessing.
The list is limited to 500 events; narrow dates if necessary. Print it using the
page's **Print activity list** button and annotate the original physical entries.
There is no compulsory per-entry completion checkbox.

Preserve the original and correction trace for reversals. A renewal is not assumed
to be cash repayment or physical redemption. Missing recipient addresses remain
explicit; source review is required instead of substituting the current customer.

## Reprints and limits

Exact reprints return the originally stored PDF. They do not contain handwritten
later annotations. Retain the physical book and its supporting records. An artifact
integrity failure needs restoration of the original stored file, not regeneration
from current loans. Source corrections do not edit old pages.

This increment supports Tamil source particulars with English headings. Reviewed
Tamil headings, unsupported legacy/renewal reconciliation, later book volumes and
complete prescribed-form coverage remain separate work. Review the applicable
physical filing and language requirements; source review and saved PDFs do not
certify legal compliance.
