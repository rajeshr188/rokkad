# Manual statutory auction notice workflow

The owner's current process is auctioneer-led: supply the proposed loans list,
receive auctioneer-prepared notices, post them manually and track follow-ups, then
send a reconciled list of loans still unreleased before sale. Preserve that division
of responsibilities. Attach the actual signed notice as printing evidence; check
its particulars against the preserved catalogue. Notices and service evidence remain
per-loan. The [auctioneer handover workflow](auctioneer-handover.md) preserves a
consolidated administrative list and reviewed remaining-loan versions for manual
sharing; it does not replace the prescribed catalogue or authorize a sale.

The in-workspace staff reference is available from **Workspace settings > Statutory
workflow guide**, **Reports > Statutory forms & notices**, and **Staff workflow
guide** on each statutory auction notice screen. It includes the steps below,
returned-post handling, corrections, attachments and the available/planned form scope.

Use **Reports → Statutory forms & notices** for the scope guide. On the loan's
Auction recovery section, a Workspace administrator opens **Statutory notice &
readiness**. Preparing a new auction leads there directly. Staff without Workspace
administration permission cannot issue, alter handling or download these records.

1. Enter/check the licensed business details, pawner's last-known address, approved
   auctioneer, approval reference, sale time/place and the loan's catalogue particulars.
   Preview, then preserve the reviewed PDF. Generation is not actual service.
2. Print and arrange the auctioneer's signature, then record the actual handling date
   and person. Reprinting retrieves exactly the preserved PDF, not today's Party data.
3. Post manually with the appropriate registration and acknowledgement/POD service.
   Record the postal article number, service, actual date, person who posted and
   booking receipt. The app does not book or send post.
4. On receipt of acknowledgement/POD, upload it and record the delivery date shown
   and actual date the proof was received. If the cover returns, record its receipt
   and endorsement instead. Record referral to the competent Village Officer,
   confirm officer receipt when known, and upload the actual official affixture/
   proclamation certificate with its dates. Never fabricate proof or mark a return
   delivered to clear the screen.
5. Review the actual postal/official service and the auction permission, schedule,
   publications, distribution and other prerequisites. Upload the supporting dossier
   and record the applicable current-rule/authority review reference. The app shows
   outstanding checks. It uses a 45-day posting interval; the official follow-up
   windows and publication dates have separate checks.
6. Start on the advertised date only when the checks pass. The existing debt/custody
   and settlement rules still apply. Completing an auction rechecks readiness too.

Late handling can be recorded honestly, but cannot clear a missed deadline. An
expired/mistaken notice requires withdrawal, cancellation and a fresh auction
arrangement; previous records remain visible. Exact-date checking also prevents an
unannounced postponement from silently reusing the old catalogue. Future dates and
backdated readiness decisions are rejected. Actual event dates are separate from
the signed-in recorder and timestamp.

Each supporting upload must be a readable PDF/JPEG/PNG of at most 10 MB. If a form
fails validation, the browser may require selecting the attachment again. Attach
only relevant evidence. Files are private and retained; they cannot be overwritten
or deleted through this workflow. Original print files do not contain later handwritten
signatures unless a signed scan is separately attached.

Courtesy email/WhatsApp reminders remain independent. A digital Sent state, a PDF
download or an old Notifications Posted marker never establishes statutory service.
The new workflow records an administrator's review, not legal certification or
permission from a government authority. Its first catalogue has English headings;
review applicable language, current amendments and local directions before use.
Other prescribed forms and multi-pledge catalogue generation remain listed in the
[delivery plan](../plans/statutory-forms-and-notices.md).
