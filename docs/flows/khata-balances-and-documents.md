# Review khata balances and documents

Local integration is available in code; production activation requires the
separate reviewed release. Current screens review existing khata evidence and
issue documents. Servicing command forms are not delivered by this checkpoint.

Open **Khata accounts** from Workspace navigation or the Loans directory. Search
by number/name/customer code; filter series, state, association, overdue interest
or pending returns. **No licence associated** includes independent accounts.
Dates sort oldest/newest and the register uses 25 records per page. Totals cover
all filtered accounts, not just the displayed page.

Open an account for actual principal, accrued unpaid interest, due and overdue
interest. Interest accrues on the agreed limit, not just the money withdrawn.
Annual payers may have accrued interest without current dues. The opening minimum
is included where applicable. Agreed limit and unused entitlement are not debt.
These figures are a dated review, not a settlement quotation.

Expand **Collateral cover & workspace policies**, **Interest schedule**,
**Collateral custody** or **Source history** as needed. Pending outgoing items
remain physically held but cannot back another withdrawal. Same-day prices feed
a collateral-based capacity suggestion; commands must recheck all lending gates.
A missing price does not erase known debt. A financially settled account can
still require actual returns; it remains visible under the return-pending filter.

The dashboard adds khata principal to portfolio principal and counts a mixed
borrower once. Khata interest appears separately from ordinary recorded interest.
Party Loans and the new-loan borrower card include actual khata debt. Ordinary
loan reports and saved health assessments keep their own labelled scope.

With edit/export permissions and current write availability, choose a document
on the account detail and press **Issue PDF**. Opening agreements and approved
amendments are distinct from actual withdrawal/activation receipts. Choose the
specific exchange, reduction, handover, settlement or correction source, or
today's statement. Sources without preceding approved lender evidence cannot
produce a voucher claiming original agreement terms.

The issued PDF is saved privately. Use its account document link for exact
reprints; later terms, contacts, repayments or corrections do not rewrite it.
A corrected original remains preserved, with correction links in history and
beside its document. A document first issued after correction also discloses it.
Missing/corrupt saved PDFs require artifact recovery, not regeneration.

Full addresses/descriptions continue onto additional A4 pages. Print every page;
financial settlement receipts do not assert actual collateral handover. These
khata documents are separate from ordinary pawn tickets and statutory books.
They are available only through authenticated Workspace staff routes. Borrower
portal summaries expose the borrower's own balances/payment history, without
publishing staff document downloads.
