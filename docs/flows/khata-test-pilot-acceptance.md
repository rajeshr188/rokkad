## Test setup and interpretation

Use fictional borrowers, an independent KH series, a INR 1 crore agreed limit,
1% **per month**, 75% LTV, and separate monthly/annual agreements. These are test
inputs, not recommended customer terms. Payment frequency never changes rate units.
The local drill fixes time to 10 October/10 November 2026; dates in its evidence
are simulated and are not real opening or payment dates.

Keep exchange/overdue policies at their warning defaults for one pass, then repeat
relevant refusal scenarios using the owner's blocking choices. Required photos
must permit draft saving and refuse approval without actual item photos.
Approvers, cashiers and collateral releasers use the existing respective Loans
permissions; being able to view an account does not permit servicing it.

## Paper label acceptance

Print the combined label and individual item labels on **100 x 60 mm** stock at
**100% / actual size**. Turn off fit-to-page scaling. Record printer model, driver,
stock, scaling, operator and date. Check a long description, Tamil borrower text
and a pending return. Text must be complete and readable and the QR must be clear.

With an authorized signed-in staff phone, scan the paper item QR and combined
account QR. Verify the correct workspace/account and current held/returned state.
Repeat after handover; the old label still resolves the item's current custody.
An unauthenticated or unauthorized device must not reveal borrower/account data.
PDF renders and route tests do not substitute for this physical check.

## Recovery and operational approval

The local fictional drill verifies full database and private-media reconstruction,
native exact-identity preview/commit, source hashes, numbering sequences and
restricted-role servicing. It does not verify a hosted image, production backup,
off-device retrieval, real operator login or physical printer.

Before hosted activation, retain the exact image/source identity, owner-only
migration result, restricted web/worker role checks and a private full database/
media backup with independently retained checksums. Restore that exact candidate
into an isolated destination and check original workspace/borrower/operator IDs,
source documents, photos, scan routes, P/U/interest and custody. Never restore
into a populated Khata destination or waive schema/guard mismatch. See the
[servicing/recovery flow](khata-servicing-and-recovery.md).

Record who handles support and daily review of outstanding amounts, overdue
interest and pending returns. Explicitly accept the unsupported statutory/default,
fees, funding and complex correction scope before any real accounts. Independent
numbering does not certify regulatory exemption. Deployment review must verify
workspace-specific external records/processes without inventing legal conclusions.

After immutable Khata evidence exists, preserve compatible servicing. If a defect
appears, pause new origination operationally and use a reviewed compatible image
or forward fix. Do not reverse evidence migrations, delete accounts or replay cash.
The final owner acceptance should identify the named hosted test workspace,
candidate/image, completed manual rows and remaining limitations.
