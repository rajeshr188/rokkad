# Choose your loan detail layout

Open any loan and use **Page layout** at the top. You can switch as often as you
like while comparing the options:

| Choice | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| A · Tabs | Six horizontal tabs; one section at a time. This is the starting choice. |
| B · Service desk | Section navigation and payment/printing actions beside the record on a wide screen; stacked on a phone. |
| C · Expandable sections | Open several sections together and scroll through them. |
| Classic · Previous design | Restores the previous loan detail arrangement. |

Your choice is remembered for your login and workspace **in this browser**.
Another member can choose differently. A different browser/device or workspace
starts with Tabs; clearing browser storage resets the choice. If storage is
blocked, the page says so and you can still switch for the current visit.

## Find a feature

The loan number, borrower, dates, balances, warnings and main next action stay
outside the sections. Printing remains prominent after approval and on eligible
imported records. **More loan actions** holds less frequent actions such as
correction, cancellation, notices, renewal and export, according to your access
and the loan's state.

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Original terms, licence/series, product and approval review/quotes. |
| Money & interest | Collection calculations, imported balance evidence, valuation, monitoring, projections and finalized interest. |
| Collateral | Items, gross/net weight, allocation, purity, appraisal, photos, labels and storage. |
| Documents | Loan tickets, imported copies/previews, key facts, receipts, releases and ticket evidence links. |
| History | Audit changes and business events, including permitted corrections. |
| Follow-up | Notices/delivery status, auction recovery and renewal links. |

Links to a particular section open it automatically. With keyboard focus on a
tab, use arrow keys to move, or Home/End for the first/last tab. On a narrow phone,
scroll the tab row horizontally to reach the other sections.

Changing layout does not submit a form, record a payment or alter the loan. Any
loan, payment or release you actually submit is a **real business transaction**;
these are alternative views of the same production records, not practice screens.
Existing confirmation and permission rules still apply. PDF ticket design is
independent of this choice. Without JavaScript, Classic remains available.

Try the normal tasks you perform: printing, finding photos, reviewing interest,
recording a genuine collection, and checking history. Report which arrangement
helps each task and what is hard to find. All four remain until the owner decides
which to keep; there is no automatic expiry or retirement date.
