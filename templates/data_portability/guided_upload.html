Before you start

  • Use one licence and series per file; up to 20 loans and 200 rows.
  • Reconcile principal, unpaid interest and fees at a completed handover date.
  • Confirm each collateral item is in your vault.
  • Use DD/MM/YYYY date text. Enter rates as 2 for 2% per month.
  • Missing facts stay unknown. Blank unpaid interest or fees do not mean zero.
