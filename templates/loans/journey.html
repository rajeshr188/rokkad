1. Prepare and lend

Approved, but cash not yet paid and prices changed? Choose Review updated valuation on the loan or disbursal page. Compare old and current prices, lending limits, rates, fees and cash to pay. A user with edit and approval permission confirms cash is unpaid and accepts today's date and terms, then chooses Confirm updated approval. The loan keeps its number, photos and earlier approvals. Check/print the updated ticket and record disbursal separately after payment. Missing prices or excessive LTV must be resolved first; the amount is not reduced automatically. For money already paid, keep the actual date and use Record completed payout. Genuine earlier approval and issued copies remain retained evidence.
Save once, then correct the saved draft: Save draft shows Saving… while it runs. Repeated submission of that same New loan form opens the loan already saved, without creating another number or applying changed values. To make further edits, open Correct draft on that loan; you can save corrections repeatedly while it remains a draft. Preview does not create a loan. If your connection fails and you are unsure whether a save completed, check Loans before opening a fresh New loan form. A fresh form creates a separate loan. Cancel an unused draft with a reason; its number stays in history.
Daily metal prices: when your valuation policy uses metal prices, record a positive INR buying price per gram of pure metal in this workspace's Rates for each metal you lend against today. If buying and selling prices are unchanged, use Confirm price unchanged for today in Rates for each required metal. This appends a dated confirmation under your signed-in identity. Approval and disbursal use today's loan date and applicable quotes. If an unpaid draft was prepared yesterday, edit its date and save before reviewing today's terms. Never move the date of money already paid simply to pass this check.
Money already paid on an earlier date? Keep the actual date and agreed item amounts/terms. Use Record completed payout on a saved draft, or New loan with the series' completed-entry purpose. This records the agreement without paying again or inventing an original Rokkad approval. General completed entry does not require old digital price quotes. The same action selects a retained-evidence review where genuine native approval or a fully reversed native payout requires it; those reviews keep their narrower checks. Active posted origins need explicit correction. Current collateral monitoring uses current evidence separately. Recording time and staff identity remain distinct from the actual transaction date.

Know the borrower

Find the customer by name and identifying details. Keep multiple contacts, addresses and photos, choosing defaults for everyday identification. Review their other loans before creating another.

Describe every pledge

Record metal, description, quantity, total weights, purity, appraisal, principal allocated to the item and photos. Quantity means pieces; the entered weights and amounts are totals for that row. Camera controls let you choose available front or rear cameras.

Gold and silver can have different rates in the same loan. The loan's displayed monthly rate combines its item allocations. Authorized approvers can override an item's rate with a reason before approval.

Review the terms

Your licence and series determine numbering. The product defines when payments are due; policies provide valuation, rates and fees. Starter products are provided for review and activation. Buying prices are maintained by your business.

Draft → Approved → Active

A draft can be corrected or cancelled. Approval checks the evidence and freezes the terms. Print the approved ticket, then record disbursal to make the loan active. A permitted combined Review and disburse workflow performs both steps.

Disbursal records the payout; Rokkad does not send bank funds. New loans require collateral photographs. Missing borrower photographs can use a blank/default presentation.

Split a draft: on a saved draft's Overview, choose Split into another draft, or use More loan actions. Select whole collateral rows, preview the two loans, then confirm. At least one row must remain. The selected items and photos move to a new numbered draft. You need create and edit permission. A quantity of two in one row is still one row; approved and active loans cannot use this operation.

Example: ₹80,000 allocated to gold at 2% per month and ₹20,000 to silver at 4% gives ₹2,400 monthly interest on ₹1,00,000 principal. Advance interest and fees, when configured, reduce the cash paid out. Review the actual disbursal summary.

2. Manage an active loan

Collections with context

Record repayments and print receipts. The system allocates collections to the applicable fees, interest and principal. Recorded balance, interest still to be finalized, scheduled dues and the full-release quote answer different questions. Use the settlement quote when closing.

Products support single-payment, periodic-interest, flexible-payment and installment journeys. Finalize completed interest periods when required. Capitalization is available only under the applicable compound-interest policy.

A repayment alone does not close the loan or record return of jewellery. Complete the release workflow when payment and handover are finished.

Keep track of physical items

Use item photos and labels, storage placement/transfers, and physical verification to connect the record with the pledged jewellery. Reappraisals add valuation evidence without rewriting the approved contract.

Where used, funding loans track eligible pledges with a funding lender as a separate obligation and custody journey. Returning items and settling funding are checked before that funding loan can close.

Search by borrower, licence or series. Reports show active totals, origination years, collateral weights and recorded appraisal values. Loan health separates overdue payments from collateral-value risk.

What does collateral coverage mean?

Coverage compares eligible collateral value with the debt exposure used by the loan's policy. That exposure may include interest, so it is not always just the original principal. Valuation can use a metal-price calculation, an approved appraisal, or the lower of both, with freshness and custody checks.

Known value

₹1,00,000 exposure backed by ₹1,25,000 eligible value means 80% LTV. If eligible value falls to ₹90,000, there is a ₹10,000 full-coverage shortfall. A policy's LTV limit can raise a warning before a full shortfall occurs.

Unknown coverage

Rokkad cannot establish a reliable comparison: for example, a required price, appraisal, monitoring policy or current assessment is missing or stale. Unknown is neither zero value nor a safe result. The amount owed may still be known.

What to do: open the loan's valuation/health explanation, resolve the stated missing or stale evidence, then ask an authorized administrator to refresh loan health. Reports must not treat unknown loans as fully covered. Scheduled refresh and message delivery depend on the workspace's operational setup.

4. Correct mistakes without losing history

Correct a draft directly. Before disbursal, an approved loan can be returned to draft with a reason. After disbursal, use the authorized reversal workflow; dependent later events must be resolved first. Then correct and reapprove the draft before recording the replacement disbursal. The loan number is retained for that correction.

Payments, releases and renewals have their own guarded reversals. Completed events and issued documents remain evidence rather than being silently overwritten. A reversal records a business correction; staff must also reconcile any cash or physical handover.

Use cancellation for abandoned drafts or approved loans. Numbers are not recycled, and ordinary deletion of operational loans is not offered. Official ticket reprints preserve issued bytes. Newly rendered imported copies are labelled as reconstructed; retained original PDFs are separately available as source copies.

5. Bring your history with you, deliberately

Use New loan to record completed paper transactions with the series' standing terms, actual item amounts and original dates. Enter receipts and closure as they become known; different-rate principal payments require the actual item split. A supported complete closed history can become an ordinary closed loan after reconciliation. Do not record a second current payout.

Reviewed opening balances continue from a verified cutover position without inventing earlier receipts. Supported complete histories preserve actual transactions. These are ordinary loans with common servicing and monitoring, subject to supported contracts, checked records, approval, statutory and custody requirements for the chosen action.

Find retained historical closed records in the Loans list. Their Historical record label identifies source claims and unknown facts. They become operational closed loans only after financial admission; browsing does not fabricate settlement. All retained snapshots remain in Historical loans.

Older portable formats retain their limits. The supported servicing bundle can preserve wider history and exact source files; full database and private-media backups remain necessary. Arbitrary Excel loan uploads require a supported reviewed import. Reports describe the loans actually entered and admitted, so paper-only records outside Rokkad are absent from its portfolio totals.

This guide describes implemented capabilities as reviewed on 26/09/2026. It does not change your permissions or loan terms. When a workflow is blocked, read its explanation and ask your administrator rather than using another date or action to bypass it.
