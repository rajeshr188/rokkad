How to use this workflow
  1. Check catalogue particulars, preview and preserve the reviewed PDF. Arrange actual auctioneer signing and printing, then record that handling.
  2. Post manually with registration and acknowledgement/POD. Record the article number, actual posting date and booking receipt. Allow at least 45 days before the advertised sale.
  3. Attach acknowledgement/POD. For a returned cover, retain the endorsement, refer to the competent Village Officer within seven days, then retain the official affixture/proclamation certificate. Do not mark a returned letter delivered.
  4. Review permission, publications, required catalogue distribution, service and objections. The second newspaper publication needs ten clear days before sale. Record actual dates; missed deadlines require a new arrangement rather than backdating.
  5. Start only on the advertised sale date after readiness checks pass. Completed loan accounting and custody controls still apply.

Current amendments, applicable language and local procedural directions need professional/authority review. Published Rules.
