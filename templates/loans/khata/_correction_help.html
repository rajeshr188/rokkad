Correction scope & support process

Corrections preserve the original source and add a separate compensating operation. They currently require an active khata and an authorized workspace administrator, plus the relevant money or collateral permission.

  • Interest receipt: the entire recorded amount was not received, or was actually refunded in full. Record the reason and actual resolution reference. Dues are restored; principal and borrowing entitlement are unchanged.
  • Exchange: outgoing collateral has not been handed over and later operations do not block correction. Originals resume eligibility; replacements are reserved for their actual return. Current retained LTV and review checks still apply.

Payouts, collateral facts, activated revisions, settlements, completed handovers, partial refunds and corrections of corrections have no supported correction screen. Settled or closed accounts also need a reviewed support resolution.

  1. Open the exact source in History and record the khata number, operation ID, discrepancy, actual cash and physical custody.
  2. Ask the workspace administrator to review linked later operations. Only genuinely independent eligible receipts can be corrected newest first, with actual full cash resolution; do not automatically undo a chain.
  3. For an unsupported case, share the source identifiers and evidence through your private support channel. Keep the original records until a supported resolution is agreed.

Do not edit completed evidence, invent a cash payout/refund or record a physical return that did not happen. Opening this guidance or requesting support does not correct the account. Support: support@rokkad.com.
