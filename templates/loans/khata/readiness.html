Supported operations and boundaries

Khata supports staged collateral-backed withdrawals, agreed-limit interest, approved limit/rate changes, grouped same-metal exchanges, formal reduction returns and full settlement with separate physical handover.

Whole interest receipts and unhanded exchanges have bounded corrections. Payout, revision, settlement, completed handover and complex historical corrections are unavailable. Review this support boundary before opening real accounts.

Khata documents and labels are operational evidence. Khata is not included in ordinary pawn pledge books, statutory notices, auctions, renewals, funding/repledging or historical imports. An independent series does not certify regulatory exemption. Decide the applicable external records and procedures before a pilot.
