Khata series & policies

Khata numbering and interest terms are separate from ordinary loans. Independent series need no licence. No existing ordinary series or loan is changed.

Pause is reversible; retirement permanently stops new lending in that series. Existing account servicing continues. Neither action resets numbers or changes the licence association.
