Closing loans: choose the right workflow

Already closed in the paper book?

Use Record paper closures. Enter the actual date when payment and all jewellery returns were completed. Check actual collections against the book.

Record paper closures

Collecting payment now?

Open the loan and use Full release. For one combined collection covering several loans, use Release multiple loans. Complete the release, then print the individual receipt.

Release multiple loans

Paper entry in three steps

  1. Choose the actual closure date (today by default), optionally enter the book/page reference, and select up to 50 loans. Review the selection.
  2. Check the suggested collections. Payer and recipient default to the borrower; open Details for changes. A shortfall requires an authorized interest concession and reason. Principal and fees cannot be waived here; excess cash needs separate review.
  3. Select the ready rows and confirm once that payment and collateral return match the paper book. All selected rows save together. Unselected rows remain for correction; leaving the screen discards unsubmitted entries.

Dates and blocked loans

One closure date applies to the whole submission. Use separate submissions for different days. Dates cannot be in the future or before the loan, and imported loans must be serviced after their migration opening date. Later financial or collateral activity blocks an earlier closure. Review such loans individually; do not change the date just to bypass a warning.

For native loans, completed interest periods must be finalized before release, as in the ordinary release workflow. If someone has not yet received all jewellery, use the repayment workflow and record the release when handover is complete.

Checking and correcting

Closure history shows the original count, actual collections, effective date, entry time and staff member. Download the paper-batch CSV to reconcile against the book; check any rows marked Reversed. A reversed receipt remains in the original total as evidence. Corrections require an authorized individual release reversal; completed records cannot be edited or deleted. Printing is optional and no automatic customer messages are sent.

The CSV is a reconciliation report, not a restorable migration package. The existing loan-history/opening restore formats cannot yet represent paper handovers with an unknown time; those exports explain the limitation rather than omit evidence. Full database backups retain the records.

Moving away from paper

The workspace owner chooses a system-first date. Use Rokkad directly for new closures from that date, clear and reconcile the earlier paper backlog, then retire routine paper entry. Administrators retain reason-required exception entry for late discoveries or outages. Other branches are unaffected.
