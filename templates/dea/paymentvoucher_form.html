Payment Voucher Guide
About Payment Vouchers

Payment vouchers track actual cash movements and automatically post to accounting following IFRS 9 standards.

Tip: Payment vouchers should only be created when cash actually moves, not when contracts are created.
Key Points:
  • Each payment automatically posts to accounting
  • Multi-currency is supported
  • You can track principal, interest, and fees separately
  • Reference numbers help with bank reconciliation
  • Draft payments can be edited or deleted
  • Posted payments cannot be edited (use reversals instead)
Payment Direction:

Receipt: Money coming IN (customer pays you)
Payment: Money going OUT (you pay supplier/lender)

Payment Methods:
  • Cash - Physical cash payment
  • Bank - Bank transfer/wire
  • Cheque - Cheque payment
  • UPI - Digital payment
  • Card - Card payment
