Understanding the Ratios
Current Ratio

Measures ability to pay short-term obligations. A ratio above 2.0 indicates strong liquidity.

Quick Ratio

Similar to current ratio but excludes inventory. Tests immediate liquidity without selling inventory.

Debt to Equity

Shows how much debt is used to finance assets relative to equity. Lower is generally better, indicating less financial risk.
