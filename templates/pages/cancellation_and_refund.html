Cancellation and refund policy

This page explains the current cancellation and refund posture for Rokkad subscriptions and implementation services. Final commercial terms may depend on the plan or agreement signed with a workspace owner.

Subscription cancellation

Workspace owners can request cancellation of paid services before the next billing cycle. Access may continue until the end of the paid period unless otherwise stated in the subscription agreement.

Refund review

Refunds are reviewed case by case for duplicate payments, billing mistakes, or service issues that prevent normal use. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment channel where possible.

Implementation and migration work

Custom setup, migration, or advisory work may have separate terms because effort begins before the workspace is fully live.
