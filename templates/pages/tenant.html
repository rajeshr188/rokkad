Workspaces

Each business runs in its own workspace.

Rokkad separates platform account management from tenant ERP workspaces. Users can belong to multiple workspaces, switch intentionally, and keep business data scoped to the active company.

Tenant isolation

Workspace data is kept separate so one business cannot accidentally see another business's loans, inventory, accounting, or team records.

Team controls

Owners can invite members, assign roles, and keep settings separate from daily ERP operations.

Clear switching

The product direction keeps workspace identity visible so users always know which business context they are inside.
