FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Rokkad's SaaS ERP direction.

Is Rokkad only for jewellery and gold loan businesses?

The product is especially shaped around jewellery, gold loan, commodity, inventory, sales, purchase, and accounting workflows, but the workspace model can support other small businesses with similar controls.

Can one user access multiple businesses?

Yes. Users can belong to multiple workspaces, and the product direction makes workspace switching explicit.

Does accounting run separately from operations?

Accounting is the core. Business documents should connect to vouchers, ledgers, inventory, commodity impact, and audit trails.
