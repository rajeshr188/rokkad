Operating the platform

This console is for the signed-in platform administrator. A workspace Owner or Admin manages their own business and does not gain access to this console.

  1. Review the overview. Look for trials approaching expiry, workspaces without subscriptions and invitation delivery problems. These signals include test records and historical email outcomes.
  2. Find the workspace. Search by name, slug or owner email. Read its lifecycle and current access separately: an active workspace can still have read-only subscription access.
  3. Understand the problem. Check owner email verification, invitation outcomes and dated access decisions. Use the onboarding report for account/trial progress. An accepted invitation does not prove the person is still a member.
  4. Use the existing workflow. Open workspace settings, team or billing. For an extension, use Manage access extension and supply its reason and expiry. Suspending a workspace is a deliberate operational decision, separate from subscription expiry.
  5. Verify the result. Return here and refresh. Review audit records when investigating a change; never replace payment evidence by editing a status directly.

Access and support boundaries

The console reads account and workspace metadata. It does not impersonate customers or display their borrower records, loans, uploaded files, invitation links or credentials. Opening a workspace uses your own identity and the existing audited access checks.

Suspend or restore a workspace

From a workspace overview, choose Review suspension for an active workspace or Review restoration for a suspended one. Check the owner and access impact, enter a reason, type the workspace slug, and confirm. The review expires after 15 minutes; changed details require another review.

Suspension blocks business screens for all members, including the owner. Records remain intact. Restoration applies the current subscription and any administrator access decision: it may restore read-only access. Neither action grants paid access, extends a trial, cancels billing or sends email. Access history records who acted, when and why. Archiving and ownership transfer remain separate workflows.

Email problems

A bounced, complained or suppressed recipient needs review before another send. Unknown acceptance must not be blindly retried. Follow the private mail-operations runbook for queue or worker failures; this console does not claim to show server or provider health.

Advanced administration

Existing Django-admin reports and audit views remain available. Their presence is not a replacement for the service-backed lifecycle, ownership and billing workflows. Delegate no new platform permissions as part of routine customer support.
